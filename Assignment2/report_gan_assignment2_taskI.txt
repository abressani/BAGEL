TRAINING SETUP
  Data: 8000 real GalaxyMNIST images (train split), 64x64 RGB, scaled to [-1, 1].
  Augmentation: random horizontal and vertical flips = True. No class labels are used.
  Generator (DCGAN, 3576707 parameters): noise vector of length 100 ->
    4 transposed-convolution blocks (4x4, 8x8, 16x16, 32x32; BatchNorm + ReLU) -> 64x64 RGB (Tanh).
  Discriminator (2765569 parameters): 4 strided convolutions (64 -> 4 pixels; LeakyReLU 0.2, BatchNorm) -> 1 logit.
  Base width 64 channels in both networks; weights initialized N(0, 0.02).
  Loss: binary cross-entropy. The discriminator sees real images with label 0.9 (one-sided label smoothing) and generated images with label 0;
    the generator is trained to make the discriminator output 1 for its images (non-saturating loss).
  Optimiser: Adam, lr=0.0002, betas=(0.5, 0.999) for both networks; one update of each network per mini-batch.

LIMITATIONS OF THE SYNTHETIC IMAGES
  - Small, low-resolution training set: 64x64 images (downsampled from 224x224) of a few thousand galaxies,
    so fine structure such as faint spiral arms, bars and compact companions is lost or invented, and
    generated images can show blur, checkerboard artifacts or blobs that are not galaxies.
  - Unconditional model: the classes (round, cigar, edge-on, spiral) cannot be requested, and the class
    mix of the generated images is unknown and may differ from the real one.
  - GAN training is unstable and the losses are not a quality measure. Mode collapse (many similar
    images) or memorization of training images are possible; the diversity number above is only a
    crude check. No realism metric such as FID is computed; quality is judged by eye.
  - Not physical: the images are RGB composites without calibrated photometry, noise or point-spread
    function, so generated galaxies have no measured sizes, colors or masses and must not be used
    for scientific measurements.
  - Detailed features (spiral arms, disk thickness) are difficult to reproduce
  - Single training run and seed; another seed or more epochs can give different samples.
