off and size of the galaxy. For example, a small edge-on disk 
has a similar profile as a large smooth cigar. By collapsing to 1D, 
we introduce this degeneracy.

Our example shows two similarly sized galaxies from different
 classes. The slopes have a similar fall-off, making it difficult 
to distinguish them from radial profile alone. The plots have the 
same normalization, radial binning, and spatial scale.
